Built-In Safety Modules of Private EA

Every algorithm setting is modular and customizable to match institutional risk tolerance on MetaTrader 5.

Fixed-Lot Progression

Replaces catastrophic 2x exponential scaling with mathematically calculated fixed-step increments, keeping total volume under strict governance.

Equity Floor & Max DD %

Hard stop parameter that terminates active baskets if an account-defined maximum drawdown percentage or absolute currency floor is ever reached.

Recovery & Range Compression

Calculates dynamic take-profit compression zones, allowing open positions to break even or exit with consolidated profit during minor retracements.
